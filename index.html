Privacy Policy

Platterhead (bundle id guru.parso.tonearm) · Last updated November 4, 2026

Platterhead collects nothing. There is no Platterhead server, no account, no analytics, and no advertising of any kind.

Platterhead is a free, open-source music player. This page explains, plainly, what the app stores and who it talks to — and it talks to no one unless you explicitly ask it to.

No account, no Platterhead server

You never sign in to Platterhead itself. There is nothing for Platterhead to collect, because there is no server it belongs to for any data to go to.

What stays on your device

  • Your music library metadata (titles, artists, play history, favorites, playlists) is stored in a local database on your device.
  • Local music files are referenced in place using a secure, sandboxed bookmark — never copied into the app, never uploaded anywhere.
  • Credentials and sign-in tokens for any remote library you connect (server passwords, archive.org credentials, OAuth tokens for cloud-drive providers) are stored only in your device's Keychain, protected by iOS. Platterhead never sees or stores these anywhere else.
  • Streamed audio from a remote library is kept in a temporary, automatically-evicted cache so recently played music still works offline. You can clear this cache at any time in Settings.

Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your library metadata, playlists, favorites, play history, custom artwork, and settings sync through your own iCloud account — never a Platterhead server, because none exists. Streamed cache audio is never uploaded, and local files stay on your device (other devices show them as "not on this device" until you re-import them there).

Who Platterhead talks to — only when you ask it to

Platterhead makes a network request only when a feature you've used requires it:

  • Jamendo (jamendo.com) — for the built-in Creative Commons mood-starter library and any Jamendo genre library you add. No account or credentials required.
  • archive.org — for any public item, list, or collection URL you paste in. Private lists require your archive.org username and password, stored only in Keychain as above.
  • Apple's iTunes Search API — to look up missing cover art for tracks that don't already have embedded artwork.
  • Any remote library provider you explicitly connect: Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, pCloud, Subsonic/Navidrome, WebDAV, Jellyfin, Plex, or an SMB share. Each connection is made directly between your device and that provider — Platterhead is never an intermediary, and your files are never routed through, or stored on, any Platterhead server. OAuth-based providers (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, pCloud) use the standard OAuth sign-in flow for that provider; Platterhead only ever requests read-only access.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Platterhead contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind — not of you, and not across other apps or websites. This matches the app's published Apple privacy manifest: no tracking, and no data types collected.

Children's privacy

Platterhead does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.
