## Description

Platterhead is a privacy-first music player for people who own their music.

Your files are referenced in place: never copied, never uploaded. Add a folder, connect your own
server or cloud drive, or paste an archive.org link and stream it. Music you have played is cached
as you listen, so it is simply there when you are offline.

MIXES THAT EXPLAIN THEMSELVES
Pick a shape — build energy, warm up and cool down, wind down — and Platterhead orders your own
tracks by tempo, key and energy, then shows you why: Why This Mix, Why This Transition, a tempo and
energy arc, and an Audition for every hand-off. Lock a track, swap one out, or choose a plain fade
whenever you prefer.

FIND MUSIC BY HOW IT SOUNDS
On-device sound search finds tracks by mood or description ("warm analog pads") without sending a
thing to a server.

EVERYTHING, FREE
FLAC, Opus, ALAC, MP3, AAC and WAV/AIFF · gapless · 10-band and parametric EQ · ReplayGain ·
crossfeed · bit-perfect output · smart playlists · tag editor · duplicate detection · listening
stats · lyrics · scrobbling · iCloud sync through your own account · widgets, Shortcuts, Siri and
CarPlay · Apple Watch downloads for offline listening.

REMOTE LIBRARIES YOU CONNECT
archive.org, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, pCloud, Subsonic/Navidrome, WebDAV, Jellyfin, Plex,
SMB and Jamendo's Creative Commons genre libraries.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No analytics. Platterhead talks only to the services you connect.

Platterhead is free software (GPLv3 or later). An optional one-time contribution in Settings
unlocks nothing — it just says thank you.

## App Review notes

- No account or sign-in is needed. Every feature is free; the single in-app purchase
  (`guru.parso.tonearm.support.dev`, consumable) unlocks nothing.
- To hear music immediately, onboarding offers verified public-domain recordings and Subsonic /
  Jellyfin demo servers.
- Sound search downloads its on-device model through On-Demand Resources on first use.

## Privacy nutrition label

Data Not Collected. The app makes network requests only to services the user adds (and to
Apple's iTunes Search for missing artwork, which sends no user identifier).
