Platterhead on Apple Watch — redesign

Built around one rule: the watch is a remote and a pocket player, not a library browser. Now Playing is the home of the app. Every screen says where the audio comes out of, and when something fails it says what failed and gives you a button to fix it. The written spec is ../DESIGN.md. Frames are a 45 mm watch (198 × 242 pt). The same kit is used for Voxglass; only the accent colour changes.

Replaces the carousel list. The top card is the player in miniature and is always there when anything is playing on either device — including music started on the iPhone. Below it are exactly four destinations. The connection state is a quiet chip, not a banner pushing content down.

2 · Now Playing — one fixed screen, Crown is volume

No scrolling, no sheet with "Close". It's a full-screen page pushed from Home (swipe back from the left edge). Artwork colours tint the background, so it doesn't need a large thumbnail. Controls are big round buttons: play/pause 58 pt and skip 44 pt (the current controls are bare 26 pt glyphs). The Crown only changes volume, shown by the green bar on the right edge. A bottom toolbar holds everything secondary.

4 · Honest states — every failure names itself and offers a fix

One Problem Card component: icon, a plain title, one sentence on what to do, one primary action, and the diagnostic code in small mono type at the bottom. It replaces the transport in place, so the screen doesn't jump. These states apply the repo's "no silent work" rule to playback.
