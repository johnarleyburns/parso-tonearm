# Basic two-deck DJ mockup notes

Companion notes for [`dj-basic-two-deck-mockups.html`](mockups/dj-basic-two-deck-mockups.html).

## Waveforms

- Each deck keeps its track title and artist in the waveform header.
- The minimap sits at the top right of that header.
- Elapsed and remaining time are centered along the bottom edge of the waveform.
- BPM is anchored at the bottom left of the waveform and includes the `BPM` label.
- Deck A remains amber and Deck B remains blue; the waveform arrangement is identical in the
  Deck A-selected and Deck B-selected states.

## Deck controls

The switchable middle section keeps only the transport and cue actions needed for this basic
surface. Its primary row is:

`PLAY` · `CUE` · `LOOP IN` · `LOOP OUT` · `LOOP`

`LOAD` remains on the following row. `CUE PFL`, `SYNC`, and `MASTER` are removed from the
switchable deck-control panel; headphone cue remains represented in the always-visible mixer
channel strips.

The mixer, hot-cue pads, jog wheel, waveform coloring, and Load-a-track sheet are otherwise
unchanged.
