Disconnected — stale data, never zeros

§42B.1 / §39A — Apple Watch performance remote. PerformanceRemoteView ▸ RemoteModel ▸ existing WCSession plumbing. Three states, because the failure state is the one that matters. A DJ performing from a phone has one hand on the device and one elsewhere; the two things they need without looking are is it still recording and how long is left, and both are glanceable. Deck time shifts amber under 60 s and red under 20 s. Telemetry arrives at ~2 Hz via updateApplicationContext — coalescing and lossy, which is correct; a wrist does not need 60 fps deck time and the battery cost would be absurd. The Watch is never in the audio path. The third frame is the whole design philosophy: when the session drops, the phone keeps performing and recording exactly as it was, and the Watch shows stale data with a timestamp rather than zeros (AT-WATCH-1/2). Stopping the recording needs a firm confirmation, because the worst possible outcome is ending a set's recording with a sleeve. Deliberately absent: waveforms, EQ, crossfader, stem faders — a wrist is a status surface and a panic button, not a mixer (§39A.4).
