§42.6 Solo deck — performing, portrait. The iPhone runs the identical engine (FR-ENG-9); only the control surface differs. One deck in focus at full width; the other in a 72 pt strip carrying title, remaining time, BPM/key and state — enough to know it is there and to stop it. A swipe swaps focus, and the swap is a view change: both decks are always live in the engine (§42.1). The crossfader lives in the always-visible bottom bar because it is the one control you must never have to navigate to. The strip glows because Mix Assist has noticed Deck A is 32 bars from its outro phrase. Three readouts sit where a Mac would have a menu bar — thermal state, granted buffer and latency, and cue mode — because on a phone there is nowhere else to put them.
