§42.8 Stems & split cue. Split cue is the iPhone's headline hardware answer (FR-HW-3b) and gets a first-class surface rather than a settings row — pre-listening is the one thing a DJ cannot work without and the one thing a phone does not natively provide (§44.2a). The diagram is deliberately prominent: §50.1 rates unfamiliarity, not technology, as the risk here, and the mitigation is one picture and one sentence. The honest cost — your master is mono — is stated on the screen where the trade is made, not buried. Stem faders are sized for thumbs and mutes quantize to the bar with a haptic (NFR-A11Y-3).
