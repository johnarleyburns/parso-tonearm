§41.6 Auto-playlist — the brief. PlaylistBriefView ▸ AutoPlaylistModel ▸ PlaylistGenerator (§28A). Free tier (FR-PLIST-1..8). The parse is inspectable and editable as chips (§28A.6) — the extractor is ~200 deterministic lines, and everything it doesn't recognise still reaches CLAP unchanged, so an unparsed brief degrades to good vibe search rather than an error. There is no language model in this product, on-device or otherwise (§28A.6). Arcs are mapped through the library's own energy CDF (§28A.5), which is why "euphoric" works for an ambient collection and a techno one. The generated state is a separate file — 05b-autoplaylist-result.html.
