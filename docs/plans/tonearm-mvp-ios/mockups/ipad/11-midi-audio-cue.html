§41.13 MIDI, audio & cue. HardwareView ▸ HardwareModel ▸ MIDIService, AudioRouteService. Pro (FR-HW-1..5). Two things distinguish this from the v1.0 Mac screen. First, there is no device picker — on iOS the route is observed, not chosen (§44.2), so the screen reports what is connected and what it can do. Every latency figure is the granted value read back from AVAudioSession (FR-SESS-2), and the Bluetooth warning states the measured cost rather than silently allowing or silently refusing (FR-SESS-4). Second, cue monitoring gets a first-class panel with a cable diagram (§44.2a) — it is the one thing a DJ cannot work without and the one thing a phone does not natively provide, and §50.1 identifies unfamiliarity, not technology, as the risk.
