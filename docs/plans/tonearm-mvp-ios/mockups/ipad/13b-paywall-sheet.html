§41.16 Paywall — purchase sheet. FR-STORE-5. The price, the permanence, Family Sharing and Restore, stated plainly. Three things here are normative rather than decorative. The green panel is the §2.4 promise — nothing free is being taken away — and it names the CI test that enforces it (Appendix T.5), which is what lets the claim be made credibly rather than hopefully. The GPLv3 paragraph is Appendix Q.4: the entitlement check is four legible lines and the source is public, so the paywall admits you can remove it — a product that says that is making a claim about what's on the other side. The single 10-minute trial is the only thing FR-STORE-6 permits, offered once.
