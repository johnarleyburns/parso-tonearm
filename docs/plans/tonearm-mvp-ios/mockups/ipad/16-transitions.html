The five transitions

Everything here works on the surface you already have — nothing to turn on.

1 Bass Swap

Bring the new track in with its LOW killed — two basslines at once is what mud sounds like. On the phrase boundary, swap them: outgoing LOW down, incoming LOW up, in one movement. The crowd hears one continuous low end.

CH A · LOWCH B · LOW channel fadersphrase ribbon → find the boundary

2 Filter Transition

Sweep the outgoing track's FILTER clockwise into high-pass — the bass thins out and the track lifts away — while the incoming comes up underneath it. Release both to centre when the swap is done. Forgiving, and it works even when the phrasing isn't perfect.

CH A · FILTERCH B · FILTER channel faders

3 Echo Out

Turn ECHO on over the outgoing track, then cut its channel fader. The track stops but its tail keeps ringing — one bar, then two, fading — and the new track walks in underneath. The exit that buys you time when a track has no outro.

Beat FX · ECHObeat length CH A fader → 0

4 Fader Cut

No blend at all. On the downbeat, cut — crossfader or channel fader, straight across. Set the crossfader curve to sharp first. The waveform's heavy downbeat tick is your visual metronome; land on it.

crossfader · sharpchannel faders downbeat ticks

5 Blend / Mix

The long one. Both tracks playing together for 16–32 bars, EQ trimmed so they don't fight — usually pulling MID on one of them. Watch the two waveforms on the shared playhead: when the grids line up, you're in phase.

§41.18 The transition coach · FR-TRANS-6. TransitionCoachView ▸ TransitionCoachModel. Free. The product's competence test is not a feature list — it is whether someone who has learned to DJ can walk up and perform. §35B fixes that target on the five transitions taught as the beginner core, and this screen teaches them against the real surface: selecting a transition highlights the actual controls it moves, in their actual positions, rather than depicting them in an illustration that will drift from the app. Three rules. It never takes over — the panel is dismissible and both decks keep playing (the §42.7b drawer discipline). It never performs the transition; an app that auto-mixes teaches nothing, so there is no "do it for me" affordance anywhere in this surface. And it is free, available before purchase, because it is the honest demonstration of what Pro actually does. Why these five, and what they cost us. Four of them — Bass Swap, Filter, Fader Cut, Blend — are performable by the M4 engine exactly as shipped; what they lacked was ergonomics and display, which is why §41.9b's relayout and §26A's waveforms are in the same milestone. Only Echo Out needed new DSP: the §35A post-fader beat-synced delay, positioned after the channel fader specifically so its tail survives the cut. That placement is the whole design — a pre-fader echo dies with the fader and the transition collapses into #4.
