§41.9 + §41.9b DJ workspace — revised in M5. WorkspaceView ▸ WorkspaceModel ▸ PerformanceEngine, TelemetryPump. Pro. What changed from the M4 layout, and why. M4 shipped a centre column laid out to fit — two EQ groups stacked as "DECK A · EQ" over "DECK B · EQ", filters as horizontal sliders in a separate row, four pads, no channel strip, no tempo fader. It was legible and it did not transfer. This revision adopts the two-channel club-controller conventions (DDJ-FLX4 class, paired with rekordbox) that FR-TRANS-2 makes a requirement: per-channel vertical strips reading TRIM → HI → MID → LOW → FILTER above a vertical channel fader and a CUE button; the crossfader horizontal and bottom-centre; CUE to the left of PLAY at each deck's inner base; the jog centred with the tempo fader on the outer edge; and eight performance pads under a mode selector. The mixer column widens 268 → 320 pt and each deck column narrows to ~416 pt — the jog module's 328 pt still fits (§41.9b). The waveforms are the other half. Per §26A: a full-track overview with a labelled phrase ribbon (bar counts, not seconds; the dashed OUTRO is a low-confidence boundary shown honestly rather than hidden), and beneath it the two decks' frequency-coloured detail waveforms stacked on one shared playhead — blue below 200 Hz, amber to 2 kHz, pale above, the same crossovers as the EQ, so when you pull LOW the blue is what leaves. Beat ticks with heavy downbeats, a hot cue marker and an active loop region. All of it renders from persisted analysis (§19.4), never from placeholder geometry. Beat FX below the crossfader is the §35A post-fader beat-synced echo — one effect, because Echo Out is the one transition of the five the M4 engine could not perform.
