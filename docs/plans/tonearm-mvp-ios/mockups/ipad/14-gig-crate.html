§41.17 Gig crate. GigCrateView ▸ GigCrateModel ▸ StemService, CacheService, StorageBudgetService. The bridge between free and Pro. Any playlist — including one the generator made — becomes a gig crate, which caches audio and queues stage-3 separation for its tracks under a budget (FR-PLIST-9, FR-ANL-9). This screen is why the previous specification's 18.7 GB library-wide stem cache became 4 GB of prepared crates: separation is scoped to one night out, which is both affordable and how DJs actually work (§43.6). The green panel shows the thermal and power governor being helpful rather than restrictive (§43.7), and the amber panel is FR-LIB-8 in the flesh — a partially-cached remote track is honestly deck-disabled, because the render path never waits on a network.
