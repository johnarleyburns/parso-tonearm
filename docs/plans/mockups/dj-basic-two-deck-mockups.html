Basic two-deck DJ surface

Portrait-only, one fixed screen: top third — Deck A waveform over Deck B waveform, each with a 3-band recordbox-style coloring, beatgrid ticks, a top-right header minimap, and bottom waveform readouts; middle third — deck controls, switchable between Deck A / Deck B; bottom third — mixer controls for both decks together. What's live here

  • Deck A is playing (PLAY highlighted), with its loop active; the middle control row keeps the transport and loop actions together.
  • An active loop is shown on Deck A (LOOP highlighted); pads 1/2 (amber = hot cue), 3 (blue), 5 (red) are set, 4/6/8 are empty slots.
  • Jog wheel hub reads the loaded deck's live BPM; dashed ring is the scratch/pitch-bend boundary (MobilePlatterGestureMapper's fixed-at-touch-down radius split, plan §2).
  • Bottom third never changes with the A/B switcher — both channel strips, the crossfader, bass faders, CFX and the stereo/split control are always visible and always live.

Waveform detail

  • Bar color = dominant band per bin: ■ low, ■ mid, ■ high — sourced from WaveformPyramid.WaveformBin.bandRMS.
  • Tall ticks = downbeats, short ticks = the other 3 beats of each bar — from Deck.beatGrid.
  • Minimap (top-right of each waveform) shows the whole track at a coarser pyramid level with a viewport box over the visible zoom window.

What changed vs. Deck A view

  • Only the middle third re-renders around Deck B: CUE is the primary action (B is paused, parked on its cue point), and the loop controls sit directly beside the transport controls.
  • Deck B's headphone cue remains mixer state, shown by the HP CUE pill on the B channel strip — it is not part of the switchable deck-control row.
  • Deck B's pad bank is mostly empty (fresh load) — pad 1 (blue) and pad 4 (red) are the only hot cues set so far.

Why this and not two side-by-side deck panels

  • The user's spec is explicit: deck controls are switchable, not duplicated — one full-size, comfortably-tappable control set per screen rather than two half-width cramped ones, matching "basic (BASIC)."
  • The waveforms above and the mixer below already show both decks at all times, so nothing about either deck's audio state is hidden by the switch — only which deck's hands-on controls are in reach.

Load a track — My Music reused, LOAD instead of PLAY

States shown

  • ON A (dimmed, non-interactive) — the track already loaded on the deck you're not currently targeting; tapping it again would reload A, so it reads as status, not action, to avoid an accidental double-load mid-mix.
  • LOAD (blue, Deck-B tinted) — the sheet was opened from Deck B's LOAD button, so every enabled row targets B; the target chip at the top states this in words the whole time it's open (CLAUDE.md's no-silent-background-work rule — the destination is never ambiguous).

What happens on tap

  • Sheet dismisses immediately; the target deck's waveform strip shows a brief "loading…" state (decode + analysis-cache lookup, not a silent multi-second freeze) before the real waveform/BPM/beatgrid populate — per DJLoadTrackSheetTests, plan §4.
  • The LOAD action never calls player.play — the app's regular Now Playing / mini-player is untouched by anything that happens on the DJ tab.
