Mixes, and the award design bar

Seven screens for plan tasks A5 and M4–M8. Every screen has to work in both appearances and at accessibility text sizes. Use the toggles: rows re-flow instead of truncating, and meaning is always carried in text, never by color alone.

Design bar shown here (plan §1)

  • Text tokens scale: switch to AX and rows re-flow rather than truncating.
  • Semantic colors in both appearances; the light accent is darkened to #9A6420 for AA contrast.
  • The style is always spelled out on chips ("Blend", "Phrase fade", "Plain fade"), never icon or color alone.
  • 44 pt minimum targets everywhere.

What's intentionally absent

  • The DJ tab, decks, jog wheels, Crate.
  • The splash screen (A4); launch goes straight to Listen.
  • Bass-swap EQ and stems (plan §4).
  • iPad and Mac: iPhone and Apple Watch only (plan C3).

Sample data

English is shown here; every string also ships in zh-Hans, es, fr, de, ja and pt-BR (plan A9), so layouts must survive German length and CJK line breaking.

Track names, artists and numbers are invented to exercise the explanations: one unavoidable tempo jump, one grid still downloading, one key-less track and one un-analyzed track.
