RootView's Listen / My Music / Settings destinations, re-laid-out for a resizable window with a mouse/trackpad instead of a thumb. Now Playing moves from an overlay dock to a persistent bottom bar with a real scrubber — there's screen space for it now.

3 · Settings → iCloud Sync — the index-sync detail

Extends the existing iCloud Sync card (Sources/Features/Settings/SettingsView.swift) with a second, index-specific block — CLAUDE.md's "no silent/magic background work" rule means the accept/reject/requeue behavior from plan §4.3 needs to be inspectable, not just correct under the hood.

Music, playlists & settings across your devices, using your own iCloud
Sound Index Sync
Share indexing results between your devices — never audio, never the raw file, just the sound-similarity data once a track finishes indexing

ⓘ

This device's own indexing always wins. If a track is already indexed here, an incoming copy from another device is discarded. An incoming embedding made with a different model version is discarded too, and that track goes back in this device's queue so it re-indexes with your current model.

4 · The accept / reject / requeue flow, traced

Same three outcomes DiscoveryEmbeddingSyncDecision.decide(...) (plan §4.3) returns, shown as what actually happens on each device for a single track.
