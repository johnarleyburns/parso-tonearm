Find, promoted from a sheet
to a real front door

Today "Find by sound, BPM or key" is a small button buried in My Music that opens a modal sheet titled "Find Music" with a "Done" button — it reads like a popup, not a destination. These three states show it living in the tab bar instead: browsing by default, an active semantic + filter search, and the specific empty/loading states that made it feel broken when nothing was explained.
